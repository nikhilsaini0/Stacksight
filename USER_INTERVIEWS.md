# User Research

## Target Personas

### 1. Sarah — Startup CTO
- **Company**: Series A SaaS, 20 engineers
- **Pain**: "I know we're probably overspending on AI tools but I don't have time to audit every subscription"
- **Behavior**: Approves tools quickly, rarely reviews plans quarterly
- **Quote**: "We just put everyone on the Team plan because it was easier than figuring out who needs what"

### 2. Marcus — Engineering Manager
- **Company**: Series B fintech, 8-person team
- **Pain**: "Finance keeps asking me to justify our AI tool budget and I don't have good data"
- **Behavior**: Uses 3-4 AI tools daily, signed up for most of them
- **Quote**: "I signed us up for Cursor Business but honestly Pro would probably work fine"

### 3. Priya — Head of Operations
- **Company**: Seed-stage, 12 people total
- **Pain**: "Every dollar matters and I'm not sure if these AI subscriptions are the right level"
- **Behavior**: Reviews all invoices, flags unusual charges
- **Quote**: "I just want a simple answer: are we overpaying?"

## Key Pain Points
1. **Visibility Gap** — Teams don't know total AI spend across tools
2. **Plan Confusion** — Unclear when Team/Business/Enterprise features justify the upgrade
3. **Seat Waste** — People leave, seats remain active
4. **Default Inertia** — Teams stick with whatever plan they signed up for initially
5. **No Comparison Tool** — No easy way to compare equivalent features across vendors
