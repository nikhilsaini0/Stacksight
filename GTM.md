# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target Audience

### Primary: Startup CTOs & Engineering Managers (5-50 person teams)
- Managing 3-8 AI tool subscriptions
- Spending $500-$5,000/mo on AI tools
- Under pressure to optimize burn rate

### Secondary: Finance/Ops Leaders
- Consolidating SaaS spend reports
- Need data for board presentations

## Distribution Channels

### Organic (Phase 1)
1. **SEO** — Target "AI tool pricing comparison", "ChatGPT vs Claude pricing", "AI spend optimization"
2. **Shareable Audits** — Each audit generates a unique URL with OG tags for social sharing
3. **Twitter/LinkedIn** — Share anonymized audit data ("The avg 10-person startup overspends $4,280/yr on AI tools")
4. **Indie Hacker / HN** — Launch on ProductHunt, share on Hacker News

### Paid (Phase 2)
1. Google Ads on AI tool comparison keywords
2. Sponsorships on engineering podcasts
3. LinkedIn ads targeting CTOs and VPs of Engineering

## Launch Plan
1. **Soft launch** — Share with 20 founder friends for feedback
2. **Product Hunt** — Launch with demo video and audit examples
3. **Content marketing** — "2024 AI Tool Pricing Guide" blog post
4. **Referral loop** — Add "Powered by StackSight AI" to shared audit URLs

## Competitive Landscape
- **No direct competitor** for AI tool spend auditing
- Adjacent: Vendr (SaaS buying), Zylo (SaaS management), Cledara (subscription management)
- Differentiation: AI-specific, instant free audit, no sales call required
